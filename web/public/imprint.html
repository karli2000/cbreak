About the project

cbreak is a private, non-commercial open-source project under the MIT license. It is free to use, shows no advertising and sells nothing. It is an independent project, not made or endorsed by Anthropic. Claude and Claude Code are trademarks of Anthropic.

Contact

  • Questions, data protection requests and legal notices: info@cbreak.dev
  • Bugs and ideas: GitHub issues
  • Security problems: private vulnerability reporting
  • A person in a room: /report in the chat pane, which reaches the moderators at once

Chat content

Messages in the rooms are written by the people in them, not by the operator. Moderators remove messages and people that break the room rules. Messages are deleted after 15 minutes in any case.

Privacy

How cbreak handles personal data is described on the privacy page.
